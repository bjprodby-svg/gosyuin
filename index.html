On the way

233 well-known shrines and temples, each with a written guide.

Every place on the map tells you what makes it special, what not to miss, when to go, how to get there and when it is open. Pick one, tap Set Off, and the app gives you a walking route with your progress on the Lock Screen, so the phone can stay in your pocket.

From Meiji Jingu and Senso-ji in Tokyo to Fushimi Inari Taisha and Kiyomizu-dera in Kyoto, Todai-ji in Nara and Itsukushima Jinja in Hiroshima.

When you arrive

When you are there, press a digital keepsake stamp. You have to be there.

Within about 100 meters of a shrine or temple on the map, the app invites you to press its stamp into your stamp book. Each of the 233 places has its own design. There is no way to press one from your sofa.

Your stamp book keeps every visit, shelved by prefecture, with twelve ranks and a set of badges to mark how far you have walked.

A note on goshuin

The stamps in this app are not goshuin.

They are original digital mementos, made for the app to mark that you were there. They are not copies of any shrine's or temple's seal.

A goshuin is a record of worship that you receive in person at the shrine or temple office, usually after paying your respects. Nothing on a phone replaces it. If you would like one, the Learn tab explains how to ask, and we would encourage you to.

Free, without an account

No sign-up, no ads

Open the app and use it. The app is free. An optional Tip Jar lets you support development, and tips do not unlock any features.

Your stamp book stays on your iPhone

Your collection is stored on your device. We have no server and no accounts, so we never receive it.

Your location is not sent to us

It is used to show nearby places, guide your walk and confirm that you have arrived. Maps and routes come from Apple, and photos come from Wikipedia. The privacy policy has the details.

  • iPhone only. Requires iOS 26 or later. The interface is in English.
  • Background location, only on a walk. Walking guidance uses your location in the background only while a walk is in progress.
  • Independent. GosyuinMap is an independent app and is not affiliated with any shrine or temple.
