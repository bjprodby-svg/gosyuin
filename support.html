How does pressing a stamp work?

  1. Find a shrine or temple on the map in the Explore tab. The app includes 269 of them, and each has its own stamp.
  2. Go there. If you like, tap Directions, then Go, for a walking route with your progress on the Lock Screen. Taking the train? Open in Maps hands the route to Apple Maps, or Google Maps if you have it.
  3. When you are within about 100 meters, the app shows an invitation with the place's seal. Touch the seal to press it. You can also turn the phone over and press it onto your palm.
  4. The stamp is now in your stamp book in the Collect tab, shelved by prefecture.

If you tap Not Now, the invitation for that place stays quiet for the rest of the session. You can still press the stamp from that place's page on the map (Press the Stamp) while you are there.

Why can't I press a stamp from somewhere else?

Because the stamp is a memento of being there. The app checks your location on your iPhone and only offers the stamp when you are within about 100 meters of the place. There is no other way to add a stamp, and we cannot add one for you: your stamp book is stored on your device and we have no access to it.

Are the stamps goshuin?

No. The stamps in this app are original digital mementos made for the app. They are not goshuin and are not copies of any shrine's or temple's seal.

A goshuin is a record of worship that you receive in person at the shrine or temple office. Nothing on a phone replaces it. The Learn tab has a guide on how to ask for one.

I am at a shrine and nothing happened

  • Check that the place is one of the 269 on the app's map. Shrines and temples that only appear in search results do not have a stamp.
  • Check that location access is allowed: iOS Settings, then Apps, then GosyuinMap, then Location, set to While Using the App.
  • Open the Explore tab. The invitation does not appear while you are searching or on another tab.
  • Large grounds can put you more than 100 meters from the point on the map. Walk toward the main hall and wait a moment for your location to settle.

Why does the app ask for my location?

The app asks for location access While Using the App, for three things:

  • to show the shrines and temples near you on the map,
  • to guide your walk to a place you choose,
  • to confirm that you have arrived, so you can press the stamp.

While a walk is in progress, the app keeps reading your location with the screen off so the Lock Screen progress stays up to date. This stops when the walk ends.

If you turn on "Tell me when I'm near a shrine", the app asks for Always access so iOS can notice, on your iPhone, when you pass a place not yet in your book, and send you one notification. It's off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off again in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings. Your location stays on your iPhone.

Apart from these two, the app never uses your location in the background.

If you do not allow location access, the map, the guides and your stamp book still work. You will not be able to press new stamps or use walking guidance.

What happens to my data?

Your stamp book and your offering record are stored on your iPhone. There is no account, and the app has no server of its own, so we never receive your collection or your location.

When you ask for a route, your start point and destination go to Apple's Maps service to calculate it. Photos of shrines and temples come from Wikimedia Commons. They were chosen in advance, and the app only asks for those files by name, never with your location.

If you delete the app, your stamp book is deleted with it. We cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

What is an offering?

The app is free and nothing in it is locked. GosyuinMap is made independently, not by a company, and an offering is an optional way to help keep it running. You choose the amount by tossing coins into an offertory box in the app, from one to ten offerings of $1.00 each (or the equivalent in your currency), and every amount gets the same thank-you. It goes to the people who make the app, not to any shrine or temple. As a thank-you, your seal gets lacquer finishes and your book a supporter's mark; on a new iPhone, Restore Purchases on the Support GosyuinMap page in Settings brings them back. The app may ask after a stamp at most three times, and you can turn that off in Settings with Ask After a Stamp. Payments are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone, with iOS 26 or later. The interface is in English. There is no iPad or Android version.

How do I contact you?

Write to us through the contact page (it opens on GitHub and needs a free GitHub account). It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the name of the shrine or temple if your question is about one. Corrections to the guides are very welcome.
